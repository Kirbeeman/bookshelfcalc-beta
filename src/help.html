Getting started with the Shelf of Shame

The Shelf of Shame shows how many of your Kindle books you haven't read yet, what you've spent and how long they'd take to read. It's free, it runs in your web browser at bookshelf.kirbee213.tv, and setup takes about five minutes on any device.

Every device needs your Amazon email and password (the account your Kindle books are on). Goodreads is optional and only used on a computer. Each device keeps its own copy of your library; to have the same library everywhere, connect Google Drive.

On a Windows PC or Mac

Use Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Opera. The page walks you through each step; here's what it will ask.

  1. Go to bookshelf.kirbee213.tv and click Set up sync.

  2. Add Tampermonkey, a free browser add-on. Click the button the page shows, then Add to your browser.

  3. Chrome, Edge and Opera only: turn on one switch. Go to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions or opera://extensions), click Details under Tampermonkey and turn on Allow User Scripts.

  4. Install the sync script: click the button on the page, then Install in the Tampermonkey tab that opens.

  5. Sign in in the same browser to amazon.com and read.amazon.com (the Kindle reader has its own sign-in).

    Goodreads is optional: books you marked read there count as read here.

  6. Back on the page, click Sync now.

    The first sync takes a few minutes; later ones are quick.

The script updates itself through Tampermonkey. Now and then the page asks you to update it before it syncs: click Update script, press Update in the tab that opens, and come back.

Safari on a Mac: the setup doesn't support Safari yet. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox on the Mac, or use the Shelf sync bookmark instead: in Settings, drag Shelf sync to your bookmarks bar, then click it while you're on amazon.com.

On an Android phone or tablet

Use Chrome. Nothing to install: a bookmark called Shelf sync reads your books on amazon.com.

Set it up (one time):

  1. Go to bookshelf.kirbee213.tv and tap Sync from Amazon in the welcome box.

    No welcome box? Tap Settings, then Set up the bookmark.

  2. A page called Add the Shelf sync bookmark opens, and the code you need is already copied.

  3. Tap ⋮ (top right), then the ☆ star.

    The bookmark is saved, already named Shelf sync.

  4. Tap ⋮ › Bookmarks, tap the ⋮ next to Shelf sync, then Edit. Clear the URL, paste, and go back to save.

    The URL should now start with javascript:

Sync (whenever you like):

  1. Go to amazon.com in Chrome and sign in.

  2. Tap the address bar, type Shelf sync, and tap the bookmark (with the star) in the list.

  3. When the dark box says Ready, tap Send to Shelf of Shame.

    Your library opens in a new tab.

Each sync also looks up genres and page counts for up to 80 more books, so a big library fills in over a few syncs.

On an iPhone or iPad

Use Safari for every step, not Chrome or the Amazon app. Nothing to install: a bookmark called Shelf sync reads your books on amazon.com.

Set it up (one time):

  1. Go to bookshelf.kirbee213.tv and tap Sync from Amazon in the welcome box.

    No welcome box? Tap Settings, then Set up the bookmark.

  2. A page called Add the Shelf sync bookmark opens, and the code you need is already copied.

  3. Tap Share, then Add Bookmark, then Save. The name Shelf sync is already filled in.

    On iOS 26, tap ••• next to the address bar first to find Share.

  4. Open your bookmarks (the open book button, or ••• › Bookmarks), press and hold Shelf sync and tap Edit.

  5. Tap the address, delete everything in it, tap and hold, choose Paste, then tap Done.

    The address should now start with javascript:

Sync (whenever you like):

  1. Go to amazon.com in Safari and sign in.

  2. Open your bookmarks and tap Shelf sync.

  3. When the dark box says Ready, tap Send to Shelf of Shame.

    Your library opens in a new tab.

Keep using the calculator in Safari rather than as a Home Screen icon: an icon keeps its own separate copy and won't see the books you sync in Safari. There's an iPhone-only version of these steps at bookshelf.kirbee213.tv/ioshelp.

Google Drive (optional, any device)

What it's for: Google Drive is not how your books get from Amazon; the sync script or the bookmark does that. Drive keeps a copy of your library in your own Google Drive, for two reasons:

  • The same library on every device. Your computer, phone and tablet all show the same books, including page counts or prices you typed in by hand. Changes made on each are merged book by book.
  • A safety copy. Browsers can clear a website's saved data, for example when you clear your history (Safari on iPhone does it on its own if you don't open the site for about a week). With Drive connected, your library comes back as soon as you reconnect.

The calculator can only see the one file it creates in your Drive (Shelf of Shame.json), nothing else. Your library goes straight between your device and Google; no one else's server sees it.

To connect, on each device:

  1. On the calculator, open Settings.

  2. Under Google Drive, tap Connect Google Drive.

  3. Sign in to Google with the same Google account on every device and tap Allow.

  4. After that it saves by itself while you use the calculator.

    When you come back another day, tap Google Drive: tap to sync near the top to pick up where you left off.
