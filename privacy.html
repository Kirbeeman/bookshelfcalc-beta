Privacy

Kindle Library Calculator · bookshelf.kirbee213.tv · Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version: your library is yours. The calculator has no server and no database. Nobody running this site can see your books, your prices or anything else about you.

Where your library is kept

  • In your browser. By default your library is saved only in the browser you use, on your own device.
  • In your Google Drive, if you choose to connect it. The calculator then saves one file, Kindle Library Calculator.json, in your own Google Drive, so your phone and computer can share it. It goes straight from your browser to Google and back.

What the Google Drive connection can do

  • It asks only for Google's narrowest Drive permission: access to files this app created. It can't see, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • The sign-in is kept in your browser tab's memory for about an hour and is never sent anywhere else.
  • You can disconnect any time in the calculator's Settings, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/connections. You can delete the file from your Drive whenever you like.
  • Information received from Google APIs is used only to save and load your own library file, and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It isn't shared, sold or used for anything else.

Amazon and Goodreads

The optional sync script and the phone sync bookmark read your Amazon and Goodreads pages inside your own browser, using your existing sign-ins, and pass what they read to the calculator page on your device. They don't send it anywhere else and they never change anything in your accounts.

Other things to know

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and Google's sign-in script if you connect Drive.
  • The code is public at github.com/Kirbeeman/bookshelfcalc, so anyone can check what it does.
